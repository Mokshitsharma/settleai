**Description:**

SettleAI is an accountability layer for agentic commerce. A person signs a mandate saying what an AI agent may buy. The
money is held by PayPal for the job, proof is checked by rules and AI, disputes end in a liability finding that cites the
mandate, and every step is a signed receipt anyone can verify offline.
